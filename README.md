# Shopify Plus — B2B, DTC & Retail on One Platform

A self-contained HTML slide deck for merchants running wholesale, direct-to-consumer,
and a physical store where **both audiences shop in the same location**.

**Live:** https://mercedesboykin.github.io/shopify-plus-unified-commerce/

## Slides

1. Title — Three ways they buy, one platform to run it
2. The shape of the business — one location, two kinds of buyer
3. DTC — the best-converting checkout in commerce
4. B2B — wholesale is native, not an app you bolt on
5. Retail / POS — the store runs on the same brain as the website
6. **The in-store wholesale buyer** — the three real solve paths
7. One location, unlimited shelf — endless aisle & omnichannel
8. Built-in AI & new demand — Sidekick + agentic commerce
9. Proof of scale — BFCM 2025
10. The stack — close, sources, and notes

## Notes

- **No pricing is shown.** Platform fees, payment processing rates, and per-location
  POS fees are deal- and region-specific. Confirm against the current order form.
- Slide 6 states honestly that B2B catalog pricing does not natively apply inside a
  POS checkout, and presents the three viable approaches. Do not remove this — it is
  the first question a wholesale-walk-in merchant's ops lead will ask.
- All figures are attributed on the final slide.
- `noindex, nofollow` + `robots.txt` — shareable by link, not search-discoverable.

## Navigation

Arrow keys / space / PageUp / PageDown, `Home` / `End`, `F` for fullscreen.
Clickable dot nav, swipe on touch, `#n` in the URL deep-links to a slide.
Print (Cmd+P) renders one slide per page.
